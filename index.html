HPTCG Arena

A downloadable platform to play the HPTCG in both PvP and PvE modes.

Red Tag Games

A web based application to play HPTCG. The goal of the project is to have the application enforce the rules of the game. It is also designed to be played in real time or can also be played turn-based with the turns being taken without both players needing to be connected at the same time.

Certamen Cartis

Focused on the original 5 WOTC sets, this platform is fully automated and on a client (download) architecture.

HPTCG Online

Full rules engine to plat the original 5 WOTC sets playable in-browser.

There are two formats to play the HPTCG with.

Classic Format

  • WOTC Sets (BS, QC, DA, AAH, COS)
  • Rules as left by WOTC
  • Best 2 of 3 games (no sideboard)

Revival Format

  • WOTC sets (BS, QC, DA, AAH, COS)
  • All Revival sets
  • Best 2 of 3 games
  • Banned and Restricted List
  • Updated Rulings and Rulebook
  • Errata List
  • Mulligan
  • Sideboard
